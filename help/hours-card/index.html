Understanding your hours

What the Hours this season card shows for one or two volunteers and one or two students.

1 minute, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The people in the video are made up.

1

One volunteer, one student

The big number is your hours. The box shows your student's total, which is the same, since you're the only volunteer.

2

One volunteer, two students

Your hours, split by the student each shift counted for, with a box for each student.

3

Two volunteers, one student

The big number is your hours. The student's box adds up everyone who worked for them. Pending shows the family.

4

Two volunteers, two students

The big number is the whole family's hours for your students. Next to it: just yours.

5

One row per student

Each student: their total from everyone, your share, how many volunteers, and what is not paid out yet.
