Edgewood Volunteer Hub · How-to

Sign in with Google: the first time

Link your Google account once. After that, your volunteer page opens with one tap on any phone or computer. Takes about a minute.

1½ minutes, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteer in the video is made up.

1

Tap Sign in with Google

Open edgewood-volunteers.github.io. The Sign in with Google button is at the top.

2

Pick your Google account

Any Google account works. It doesn't have to be the email the Hub sends to.

3

Already on the list? You're in

If the Hub already has your Google email, your page just opens, and you're done.

If not, you'll see Welcome! New to volunteering? Tap I'm new: sign me up (the new volunteer guide walks through the rest). Already volunteering? Use the Already a volunteer? box.

4

Enter your code, then tap Link my Google account

Your volunteer code is in any email from the Hub. It looks like K7Q4-M9XD. You only do this once, and your Hub emails keep going where they go now.

Don't have your code? Tap Don't have your code? Email it to me, right under the Link button. Enter the email your Hub emails come to and tap Email my code. Your code and a button that opens your page arrive in a minute or two. Family on one email? Everyone's code comes in the same email.

5

You're in

From now on, Sign in with Google opens your page with one tap.

!

One heads-up

Once you link Google, typing just your email on the sign-in page won't open your page anymore. Use Sign in with Google, or your volunteer code.

Family members who share your email can use the same Google account, or their own code.
