Admin guide · Part 10 of 20

Manage stands

Create a Hub game, its stands and positions, from your phone. Edit it later, or delete it.

2:26, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteers, games and phone numbers shown are made up.

1

Open Manage stands

It is in the Hub games card. Managers and Full admins see it.

2

New game, or open one

Pick a game to change it, or start a new one. Then the event name, date, and when the stands open and close.

3

When sign-up opens and locks

Open sign-up at a set time, or as soon as you save. Lock closes sign-up before the game. Notes show on the board.

4

Stands and positions

Each stand gets a short code. Start from layout fills in a saved set of positions.

5

Add stands, then create

Add stand for each one. The totals are at the bottom.

6

Saved

The game is created and stays open for editing.

7

People already signed up are protected

A position can grow, but not shrink below the people on it.

8

The Hub says no

The name and date lock once anyone signs up. New times email the people on those shifts.

9

Delete a game

Only before anyone signs up. It takes a second tap.

10

From a roster

Edit stands and times opens the same form for that game.
