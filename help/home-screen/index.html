Edgewood Volunteer Hub · How-to

Put the Hub on your home screen

One tap from your phone's home screen to your volunteer page, like an app. Nothing to download from an app store.

iPhoneAndroid

Start on your volunteer page in your phone's browser: edgewood-volunteers.github.io. On iPhone use Safari; on Android use Chrome.

When you're done, this icon is on your home screen, named Volunteer Hub.

iPhone (Safari)

1

Tap the ••• button, then Share

The button is next to the address bar at the bottom of the screen. In the menu, tap Share .

On an older iPhone (before iOS 26), the Share button is right on the bottom bar. Tap it.

2

Tap Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the list a little to find it.

Not there? Scroll to the bottom, tap Edit Actions, and add Add to Home Screen.

3

Keep Open as Web App on, and tap Add

The name says Volunteer Hub; you can change it. Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add at the top right.

!

Sign in once inside the new icon

On iPhone, the home-screen icon keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari. The first time you open it, sign in again with your volunteer code (or Email me my code). After that it remembers you.

Android (Chrome)

1

Tap the ⋮ menu

The button is at the top right of Chrome.

2

Tap Add to Home screen, then Install

Some phones show Install app right in the menu; tap that instead.

Then tap Install. If your phone asks, tap Add (or drag the icon where you want it).

3

Open it from your home screen

The Volunteer Hub icon opens your volunteer page full screen. It stays signed in the same way Chrome does.

Samsung Internet: tap ☰ at the bottom, then Add page to › Home screen.

Good to know

• The icon always opens your volunteer page. If more than one person uses this phone, switch between them the same way you do in the browser.

• To remove it, press and hold the icon and choose Remove (or Delete Bookmark on iPhone). That doesn't change anything in the Hub.

• On a phone you share or borrow, don't add it. Use the browser, and tap Forget me when you're done.
