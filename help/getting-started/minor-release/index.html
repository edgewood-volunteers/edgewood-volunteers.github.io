Sign the minor release

Levy requires it for volunteers under 18. The student signs, then a parent or guardian.

1:03, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteers shown are made up.

1

Tap Sign the minor release online

It shows on the student's volunteer page while the release is missing.

2

The student signs first

Check the printed name, sign with a finger, and check the box. Then choose how your parent or guardian will sign.

3

Parent with you? Sign right here

Pick They're here with me now. Your parent enters their name, confirms you're at least 16, signs, and checks the box. Tap Sign and submit.

4

Not together? Send them a link

Pick Send it to them and tap Sign and send to my parent. Then Text the link or Copy the link. It works for 14 days, and your release files itself as soon as they sign.
