Getting started · Part 1 of 5

Sign up as a volunteer

Sign in, fill in your info (every group, every student, family manager), and save it.

1:57, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteers shown are made up.

1

Sign in with Google, or type your email

Open edgewood-volunteers.github.io. Tap Sign in with Google, or type your email and tap Open my page.

2

Tap I'm new: sign me up

The Hub doesn't know your email yet, so it offers to sign you up.

3

Fill in your info

  • Your name (turning in paperwork for a student? enter the student's name).
  • Your email is filled in and locked so it can't get mistyped.
  • Phone and shirt size.
  • Check every group your students are in, not just one.
  • List every student your hours count for, first and last name, separated by commas (for example: Riley Smith, Sam Smith).
  • Family manager: check I'd like to be the family manager for these students if you want to manage your family's volunteer credit. When credit is paid out, the family manager decides how your family's share is split between your students. One per family; the IU liaison approves it, and nothing changes until then.
4

Under 18?

Tap Yes and add a parent or guardian name and phone. Your paperwork list adds a Minor release for a parent to sign (Part 3).

5

Your paperwork list

Each document has its own part in this series. Already have signed paper copies? Add them in each card and tap Submit.

6

Tap Save my info and sign online

Open the Levy volunteer packet card and tap the teal Save my info and sign online. It saves your info and opens Levy's packet to sign.

7

Check it, then tap Yes, save it

Check every line, including Family manager. Anything off? Tap Fix something. Once you save, Levy's packet opens (Part 2).
