Getting started · Part 4 of 5

Creating Legends training

Levy's short online orientation. Every volunteer needs the certificate on file.

0:54, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteers shown are made up.

1

Tap Upload paperwork

Creating Legends shows as missing on your volunteer page until the certificate is in.

2

Take the training, then upload

Open the Creating Legends certificate card and tap Take the Creating Legends training. The certificate usually arrives by email within 5 minutes. Enter the date, upload the certificate (or a screenshot of it), and tap Submit.

3

Or attach it to Levy's packet

Signing the Levy packet online? Its last section takes the certificate too.
