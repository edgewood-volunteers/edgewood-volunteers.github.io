Getting started · Part 2 of 5

Sign the Levy packet

Every volunteer signs Levy's packet once a year. About 3 minutes, no printing.

1:04, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteers shown are made up.

1

Open the Levy packet

It opens by itself right after you sign up. Any other time, tap Sign the Levy packet online on your volunteer page.

2

Check your name, then each policy

Your name and initials are filled in. Check each policy; the full packet is linked at the top.

3

Answer, sign, and submit

Answer the health questions honestly (answer yes to any and the IU liaison will contact you). Sign with your finger or mouse, check the box, and tap Sign and submit.

4

It files itself

Your signed packet goes in with your paperwork, and the IU liaison reviews it.
