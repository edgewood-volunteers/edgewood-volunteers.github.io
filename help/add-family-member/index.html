Edgewood Volunteer Hub · How-to

Add a family member

A spouse, a student, a grandparent: anyone who shares your email can get their own spot on the volunteer list, with their own paperwork, shifts and hours. Takes about 2 minutes.

1½ minutes, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The family in the video is made up.

1

Open your volunteer page

Go to edgewood-volunteers.github.io and tap Update my info & paperwork.

2

Tap + Add another person on this email

It's right under your own paperwork status.

3

Your email is already filled in

It's grey and locked on purpose. Everyone in the family shares it, so you all get the same Hub emails. Wrong email? Tap Start over.

4

Fill in their info

  • First and last name (and a nickname if they go by one)
  • Under 18? Tap Yes, then add a parent and parent phone.
  • Phone, shirt size, and the group(s) they volunteer for
  • Student(s) your hours count for: the student who gets credit for their shifts. Two students? Separate them with a comma.
5

Upload their paperwork, then tap Submit

Open each document you have and upload a photo or screenshot. A phone photo is fine. Don't have it yet? Skip it; they can upload it later from their own page.

6

Check it, then tap Yes, save it

Your browser may have filled in something wrong, so check each line. Anything off? Tap Fix something.

If the name matches someone already on your email, you'll see a red warning. Same person? Tap Fix something, then Start over, and pick them instead of adding them twice.

7

Done: they're on the list

They now show up at the top of your volunteer page. Tap + their name to add them to this phone, then tap any name to switch between family members.

Open my volunteer page

Questions about shifts, stands or paperwork? Email the IU liaison (link at the bottom of your volunteer page).
