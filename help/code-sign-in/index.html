Edgewood Volunteer Hub · How-to

Sign in with your volunteer code

No Google account? No problem. Your code opens your page, and your phone or computer remembers you after that.

1½ minutes, with narration. Turn on captions (CC) to watch with the sound off. The volunteer in the video is made up.

1

Type your code, tap Open my page

Open edgewood-volunteers.github.io. Your code is in any email from the Hub and looks like K7Q4-M9XD. Dashes and capital letters don't matter.

2

You're in

This phone or computer remembers you. Next time, just open the page.

3

No code? Tap Email it to me

Tap Don't have your code? Email it to me (under Open my page). Enter the email your Hub emails come to and tap Email my code. Family on one email? Everyone's code comes in the same email.

4

Tap Open my volunteer page in the email

It signs you in on that phone or computer. The code is in the email too, for your other devices.

5

Switch between family members

Tap a name at the top to switch. A dotted + name shares your email: tap it to add them to this device. + Add signs in someone else.

6

On a shared computer?

When you're done, tap Forget me on this device at the bottom of the page, so the next person can't open your page. Forget everyone on this device clears the whole family.

!

Code or email?

Typing your email instead of your code shows your shifts, hours and paperwork. Your code opens everything, including your paperwork page and naming your extra spots. (If you've linked a Google account, use Sign in with Google or your code; your email alone won't open the page.)
